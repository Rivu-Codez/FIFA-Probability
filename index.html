Under the hood

How a single match gets decided

Each team's historical win, loss and draw rate (from every match it played between January 2023 and June 2026) is multiplied by a strength factor drawn from its FIFA ranking tier. A team that beat mostly Tier 1 opposition earns more credit than one with the same record against Tier 4 sides.

WinA = Win%A + Loss%B
WinB = Win%B + Loss%A
Draw = Tie%A + Tie%B

Those three weighted values become the odds fed into a weighted random draw for the match — so a heavy favourite wins most of the time, but never all of the time. Drawn group games are settled for the group table on points as normal; a drawn knockout match goes to a coin-flip shootout.

The ranking multiplier itself tapers as the tournament goes on — full strength in the group stage, roughly half-strength through the round of 32 and quarter-finals, and switched off entirely for the semi-finals and final, where the model assumes reputation stops mattering and form takes over.
